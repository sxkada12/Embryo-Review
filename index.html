High-Yield Board Pearls

  • • Kartagener Syndrome: Dynein arm defect stops leftward nodal fluid flow; causes reversed cardiac looping & dextrocardia.
  • • Diaphragmatic Hernia: Failure of left pleuroperitoneal membrane; causes compressional pulmonary hypoplasia.
  • • Canalicular Phase (Wks 16-25): Gas exchange impossible prior to ~25 wks; Type II pneumocytes arise ~20 wks.
  • • Arch Arteries: 3rd arch = common carotids; 4th = aortic arch & right subclavian; 6th = ductus arteriosus & pulmonary arteries.
  • • Chiari II: Cerebellar vermis and medulla downward displacement through foramen magnum; strongly linked with myelomeningocele.
